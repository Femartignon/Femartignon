# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Takeda **event-operations and finance operators** — the people who manage the
event lifecycle day to day: registering events, tracking POs and NFs, chasing
close-outs, and watching compliance and spend. They work inside the Central de
Controle de Eventos (FY24–FY26+) and read the Global Intelligence Grid surfaces
(the 3D globe, flight tracker, and executive brief) to understand event status,
financial exposure (≈R$ 31.5M across ~488 payments), and risk at a glance. Their
context is operational and recurring: they return often, scan fast, and need to
trust the numbers.

## Product Purpose

A single source of truth for Takeda's global event operations — turning a
Medallion (Bronze/Silver/Gold) data model into living dashboards and
spatial intelligence. The product exists to replace manual reconciliation with
self-updating KPIs, alerts (overdue close-outs, POs without NF), and a
geospatial view of where events and spend concentrate. Success is an operator
opening any surface and immediately knowing what needs attention, with no
re-derivation and no doubt about the data.

## Brand Personality

**Authoritative · precise · cinematic.** Mission-control intelligence in the
caliber of NASA SVS, Cesium, SpaceX Mission Control, and Palantir Gotham. The
voice is calm and exact — it states facts, surfaces risk, and never decorates.
Confidence comes from fidelity (real data, real geography, real money), not from
flourish. On the Takeda-branded operational surfaces, it carries the 2026
identity (red `#E1242A`, charcoal `#34373F`) with restraint and credibility.

## Anti-references

- **The Three.js demo / toy.** No neon palettes, heavy scanlines, gimmicky
  glow, or "look what the GPU can do" flourish. The dossier's own root-cause
  note stands: it must read as enterprise intelligence software, not a tech
  demo. Spectacle must always serve comprehension.
- By extension: cookie-cutter SaaS card grids, gradient hero-metrics, and
  consumer-flashy marketing treatments that would undercut pharma credibility.

## Design Principles

1. **Fidelity over flourish.** Every visual must be faithful to the underlying
   truth — real Blue Marble albedo, real terrain relief, real numbers. If an
   effect doesn't increase comprehension or trust, cut it.
2. **At-a-glance first.** An operator should grasp status, risk, and exposure
   in the first second. Hierarchy, color, and motion exist to answer "what
   needs attention?" before any detail is read.
3. **Earn the spectacle.** Cinematic rendering is allowed only when it sharpens
   understanding (geography, scale, concentration). The moment it reads as a
   toy, it has failed.
4. **Living, not manual.** Surfaces reflect the data automatically; the design
   should make that freshness legible (states, timestamps, status faróis) so
   operators trust it without re-checking the source.
5. **Quiet authority.** Calm, exact, restrained. Density is fine; clutter is
   not. The interface speaks with the confidence of a control room.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its (often near-black) ground;
status colors (the close-out/PO faróis) must not rely on hue alone — pair with
shape, label, or icon for color-blind operators. Heavy 3D and entrance motion
require a genuine `prefers-reduced-motion` path (freeze auto-rotation, swap
fly-throughs for cuts/crossfades). Internal HCP data is LGPD-sensitive and
confidential; treat it accordingly in any surface that renders it.
